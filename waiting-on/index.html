Privacy Policy for Waiting On

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Waiting On, an Android utility published by Mystery Box for tracking pending requests, applications, replies, orders, payments, appointments and other items that may need follow-up.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Waiting On Android application, package name com.mastermystery.waitingon.

2. Information handled by Waiting On

Waiting On does not require an account, sign-in, profile, name, phone number, address or payment information. The developer does not operate a backend server for Waiting On.

Information you choose to enter may include item titles, categories, start dates, expected dates, follow-up dates, contact or organization details, reference or tracking numbers, notes, completion status and the date you last recorded contact. This information is stored only in the app's private local storage on your device and is used to provide tracking, status, widget and reminder features.

3. Data collection and third parties

Waiting On does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, social SDKs or cloud-sync services. The app does not request the Android Internet permission and does not intentionally transmit your waiting-item data to Mystery Box or to third parties.

4. Notifications and reminders

If you create a follow-up date and choose to enable notifications, Waiting On may request Android's notification permission. The permission is used only to display follow-up reminders for items you create. Reminder scheduling is performed locally on your device.

You can disable reminder notifications in the app's Settings screen or through Android's application notification settings.

5. Home-screen widget

The Waiting On home-screen widget displays information from your locally stored pending items. Widget content is generated on the device. Adding a widget does not upload your item information to the developer.

Because widgets are visible on your launcher, information shown in a widget may be visible to anyone who can view your unlocked home screen. You control whether to add, resize or remove the widget.

6. Data sharing

Waiting On does not sell your information and does not intentionally share your waiting-item data with advertising networks, analytics providers or other third parties.

Information may only be disclosed if the developer is legally required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process or a valid governmental request. Because Waiting On does not operate a server containing your item data, the developer normally does not possess that local data.

7. Data retention, backup and deletion

Waiting-item data remains in the app's private storage on your device until you delete an item, clear the application's storage or uninstall Waiting On. Completed items remain locally stored until you delete them or remove the application's data.

Waiting On disables Android application backup for its local app data. There is no Waiting On user account or server-side item database to delete.

8. Security

Waiting On minimizes data exposure by storing item information locally in the application's private storage and by not providing the app with Internet access. Access to application-private storage is controlled by Android. However, no device or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so avoid entering sensitive information that is not necessary for your tracking purpose.

9. Children's privacy

Waiting On is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not specifically directed to children under 13 or the applicable minimum digital-consent age in a user's country. The developer does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

10. Your choices

You can choose what information to enter into each item, whether to set a follow-up reminder, whether to allow notifications, whether to place the widget on your home screen and when to delete an item. You can remove all locally stored Waiting On data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, legal requirements or data practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be changed.
