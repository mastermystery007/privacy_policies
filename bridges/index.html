Bridges: Hashi Logic Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

MysteryBox144 publishes Bridges: Hashi Logic Puzzles, an offline, single-player Android game. This policy describes how the ad-free release handles data.

Game data

Bridges saves puzzle progress, completed puzzles, statistics and settings on your device in Android's private app storage. Bridges has no user accounts or developer-operated backend. This release contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and sends no game or personal data to MysteryBox144.

Android backup and transfer

Depending on your Android settings, the system can back up or transfer your saved progress and preferences when you move to a new device. Google or your Android device provides these services under your settings. MysteryBox144 cannot access your backups.

Challenge codes

A challenge code lets another player recreate your puzzle. A code identifies the puzzle and may include your solve time. You decide whether to copy it or send it through another app using Android's share menu. The service you choose handles the information you send under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Bridges keeps game data on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android may restore a copy if you have backup enabled. Manage any system backup separately in your Android or Google account settings. MysteryBox144 has no server copy to delete.
