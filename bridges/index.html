Bridges: Hashi Logic Puzzles

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

MysteryBox144 publishes Bridges: Hashi Logic Puzzles, a single-player Android game. Puzzles work offline. Version 1.0.1 introduces advertising on supported devices.

Game data

Bridges saves puzzle progress, completed puzzles, statistics and settings in Android's private app storage. MysteryBox144 operates no game accounts or game servers.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob provides banners on Home and Stats. During a puzzle, you can choose to watch an ad for one hint. When the app requests ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information and device identifiers such as the advertising ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The SDK encrypts this data in transit.

Bridges uses Google's User Messaging Platform for regional privacy choices. Where available, open Settings and select Privacy choices to revisit them. You can also manage the advertising ID in Android's privacy settings. Google explains how it handles and retains ad data in its partner privacy notice and Privacy Policy.

Android backup and transfer

Depending on your Android settings, the system can back up or transfer your saved progress and preferences when you move to a new device. Google or your Android device provides these services under your settings. MysteryBox144 cannot access your backups.

Challenge codes

A challenge code lets another player recreate your puzzle. A code identifies the puzzle and may include your solve time. You decide whether to copy it or send it through another app using Android's share menu. The service you choose handles the information you send under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Bridges keeps game data on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android may restore a copy if you have backup enabled. Manage any system backup separately in your Android or Google account settings. MysteryBox144 has no server copy to delete.
