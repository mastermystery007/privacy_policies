Privacy Policy for ResumeMaxx

Last updated: 9 November 2026

How ResumeMaxx handles resume information, optional AI requests, saved reports and advertising.

1. App and developer

This policy applies to ResumeMaxx for Android, application ID com.mysterybox.resumemaxx, published by Mystery Box. Contact us at mysterybox144@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

ResumeMaxx helps you review your own resume against a role or job description. It does not require a ResumeMaxx account or sign-in.

2. Resume data on your device

You may paste resume text or select a PDF or DOCX file, choose a target role and experience level, and enter or share a job description. This information can include names, contact details, education, employers, work history and any other details you choose to include.

File text extraction, scanned-PDF text recognition and the basic and Gold Star analyses run on your device. Scanned-PDF recognition uses Google's bundled ML Kit Latin-script text-recognition model. Resume images are not uploaded for this recognition step. Google SDKs may separately process service or diagnostic information.

Reports you choose to save are kept in Android app-private storage on your device. The app disables Android backup for its app data. Delete saved reports in the app, or clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Your original imported documents and copies saved elsewhere are not deleted by these actions.

3. Optional AI processing

If you unlock Diamond Star or request AI resume tailoring, your resume text, job description, target role and experience level are sent to our backend, currently hosted on Render, and forwarded to DeepSeek to generate the requested review, suggestions and interview questions. AI review requests may also contain a summary of the on-device findings. Tailoring requests contain the resume bullets to be rewritten.

The backend attempts to remove email addresses, phone numbers and common LinkedIn and GitHub profile links before forwarding content to DeepSeek. This is not complete anonymization: names, addresses, employment history, identifying achievements and other personal details may remain. The backend receives the submitted text before this filtering. Remove unnecessary personal or confidential information before requesting AI processing.

Basic and Gold Star resume analysis do not send your resume to DeepSeek. You can decline the AI unlock. AI suggestions may be inaccurate; review every proposed change and retain only truthful information.

4. Service identifiers, network data and logs

The app creates a random installation identifier stored locally and sends it with AI requests for service operation and rate limiting. Our backend and hosting provider receive network information, including an IP address. The Node backend uses IP addresses and installation identifiers to limit abuse.

The application backend is designed to log request metadata such as route, response status, duration and cache status, rather than resume or job-description text. Render and other infrastructure providers may maintain their own access, security and operational logs under their terms. These provider practices are separate from the application's logging code.

5. Advertising and consent

ResumeMaxx uses Google AdMob rewarded advertisements to support Gold Star and Diamond Star access. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic or performance information, advertising identifiers, app-set identifiers and other applicable device or account identifiers. Purposes include advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where required. Where Google requires it, a Privacy settings control lets you review advertising privacy choices. Some advertising processing may occur when the app initializes the ads SDK, rather than only while a rewarded video is playing. The app does not intentionally provide resume text or job descriptions to the advertising SDK.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures. Actual ad processing depends on your location, consent choices and the advertising configuration.

6. Sharing, copies and exports

If you copy, share or export a report or tailored resume, the selected content is placed on the clipboard or provided to the app or destination you choose through Android's share interface. Those destinations handle the data under their own policies. Copies outside ResumeMaxx remain until you delete them separately. Temporary export files are also created in the app's cache and may remain until the cache is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

We do not sell resume text to advertisers. This does not mean that no information is shared: optional AI content goes through Render to DeepSeek, and the advertising SDK processes advertising-related information with Google and its partners. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Retention and deletion

Local saved reports remain until deleted, app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. The random installation identifier remains in app storage until that storage is removed.

The backend caches successful AI responses derived from submitted resumes and job descriptions. The current Node implementation gives each cached response a 24-hour validity period. Expired entries are removed when accessed; entries can also be evicted when the cache fills or the process restarts. This is not a guarantee that every entry is physically erased exactly 24 hours after creation. Rate-limit records are held in memory and reset or are removed as limiter windows advance and cleanup occurs, or when the process restarts.

Hosting logs and data processed by DeepSeek or Google may follow different retention periods and contractual terms. We do not promise that those providers retain no data or never use data for model improvement. Contact us for questions or deletion requests concerning service-held information. Do not email an entire resume or identity document unless necessary and requested. We will explain what information we can locate and what provider assistance may be needed.

8. International processing and security

AI requests and advertising data may be processed outside your country. DeepSeek is based in China; processing locations and safeguards depend on the applicable API agreement and provider infrastructure. Render and Google may use international infrastructure. Provider terms and available safeguards should be considered before submitting personal or confidential information.

Release builds use HTTPS for backend traffic, and the DeepSeek API key is held on the backend rather than included in the Android app. App-private storage and transport encryption reduce exposure but do not guarantee absolute security. This policy does not promise that locally saved reports are encrypted by the app.

References: Render Privacy Policy, DeepSeek Privacy Policy and DeepSeek API documentation. DeepSeek's consumer privacy policy states that downstream applications must provide their own privacy disclosures; it should not be interpreted as an API-specific promise of retention or training restrictions.

9. Your choices and rights

You control the text you enter, whether to use optional AI processing, which reports to save, and which exports to share. Use the in-app privacy-options control where available to review advertising choices. You can also use Android's advertising-ID controls.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data, object to processing, withdraw consent, request portability, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact mysterybox144@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully completed. There is no ResumeMaxx user account to delete.

10. Audience and changes

ResumeMaxx is intended for people preparing job applications, including students and working professionals. It is not designed specifically for children. Do not submit a child's information or another person's confidential resume without appropriate authority.

We may update this policy when the app's features, provider configuration or data practices change. The revised policy will appear here with an updated date.
